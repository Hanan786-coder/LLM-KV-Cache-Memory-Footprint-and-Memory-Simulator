## E4. Eviction under memory pressure

LLaMA-7B, paged-16, a 2 GiB pool (4,096 token slots), 300 `chat` requests arriving at 0.5 per step.

| policy | mode | steps | preemptions | recompute_tokens | swap_out_bytes | e2e_p50 | e2e_p95 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| lifo | recompute | 13,059 | 354 | 82,202 | 0 B | 5,365 | 11,564 |
| fifo | recompute | 13,983 | 138 | 126,954 | 0 B | 5,647 | 12,602 |
| largest | recompute | 14,795 | 109 | 137,669 | 0 B | 6,216 | 13,185 |
| lifo | swap | 12,928 | 385 | 0 | 42.97 GiB | 5,269 | 11,440 |
| fifo | swap | 13,824 | 152 | 0 | 72.87 GiB | 5,626 | 12,425 |
| largest | swap | 15,309 | 134 | 0 | 91.45 GiB | 6,402 | 13,578 |

LIFO (vLLM's choice) throws away the newest requests, which have the least work to redo; FIFO evicts the oldest; largest-first frees the most per eviction. Swap trades recomputed tokens for PCIe traffic.
